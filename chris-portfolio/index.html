About

D. Christopher Gnanadurai

Director of Product Design · India

Design leader with 18+ years of global experience building high-performing design organizations and shaping data-driven, user-centered products across supply chain, ERP, ALM, e-commerce and enterprise SaaS. Currently Director of Product Design at Resilinc, driving AI-powered design strategies that impact engagement, retention, and revenue growth for Fortune 500 customers. Recognized for transforming design from a support function into a strategic growth lever, with expertise in design systems, AI/ML integration, customer co-creation, and measurable ROI communication.

Core achievements

  • Scaled a design team from 3 to 12+ designers across India, US and UK, coaching senior ICs into managers and embedding design culture aligned with business OKRs.
  • Awards: Top Gun Leadership Award (2021), Dream Team Awards (2019, 2021), CollabNet Hackathon Runner-up (2017).
  • Helped FourKites achieve a #2 Usability ranking in its first-ever Nucleus Research evaluation.
  • Played a key role in FourKites earning Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ Leader recognition for user experience for three consecutive years (2021–2023).
  • Thought leadership: delivered conference keynotes and design thinking workshops for industry and university audiences.

Quantifiable impact

  • Increased executive engagement by 18% within 3 months through a Workspace ecosystem redesign, driving a 46% growth in upsells.
  • Improved Executive NPS by +32 points, generating 100+ product leads and accelerating upsell closures within the first month.
  • Facilitated an AI disruption management workshop with 20+ Fortune 500 executives, positioning AI as a resilience lever that reduced detention costs and accelerated time-to-resolution.
  • Designed FastTrack, a QR-based check-in enabling 100% contactless operations and reducing infrastructure costs by eliminating kiosks.
  • Spearheaded a Value Realization initiative ("ROI Wrapped") that shortened renewal cycles, reduced churn risk, and created new upsell opportunities.
  • Designed the Border Congestion Map during COVID-19, later expanded to 3 continents and cited by NYT & GlobeNewswire, helping enterprises reroute supply chains.

Leadership philosophy

I believe world-class design teams are built on empowerment, trust, and accountability. My role as a leader is to create the conditions where designers feel confident to take ownership, experiment boldly, and learn fearlessly.

I foster a culture of continuous growth — where making mistakes is not a weakness but a catalyst for innovation. My philosophy of "fail fast, fail safe" ensures teams can take risks with psychological safety, while still delivering meaningful impact at scale.

Leadership, to me, is about clarity and connection: aligning design vision with business strategy, ensuring every designer understands not just the task at hand, but the value it creates for our customers and organization.

I am committed to giving timely, actionable feedback, celebrating wins, and guiding through challenges. By instilling pride, curiosity, and a sense of shared purpose, I aim to build teams that are not only creative and resilient but also strategic partners in shaping the future of our products.

Roles & responsibilities

  • As a user advocate, effectively challenge and enhance the user experience of the product through data-driven design decisions.
  • Translate concepts into creative visual representations to clarify ideas and promote user-centered design (UCD), usability and accessibility across the organisation.
  • Recruit and lead design teams, coaching and mentoring to develop their acumen in the UX design process.
  • Provide direction to a team of UX designers; lead creative and holistic thinking on diverse products, platforms and devices.
  • Build innovative, cohesive teams that collaborate with product managers, engineering, CS, sales, marketing and customers at every stage of the design thinking process in an agile environment.
  • Establish design standards and consistency that reflect brand standards across the organisation through the governance of design systems.
  • Define short and long-term UX strategy, OKRs, and a quarterly roadmap to achieve the product design and research vision.
  • Use quantitative and qualitative methods to uncover user patterns, behaviours, and insights, translating findings into recommendations.
  • Synthesise user testing findings with internal and external stakeholders and iterate designs rapidly based on learnings.
  • Create UX benchmarks to compare the current application experience against previous versions or competitive products using significant UX metrics.

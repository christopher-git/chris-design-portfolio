Leadership

Leading with empowerment, trust, and clarity.

I believe world-class design teams are built on empowerment, trust, and accountability. My role as a leader is to create the conditions where designers feel confident to take ownership, experiment boldly, and learn fearlessly — then get out of the way.

Leadership philosophy

Six principles that shape how I lead.

Empowerment & Ownership

I create the conditions where designers feel confident to take ownership, experiment boldly, and learn fearlessly.

Trust & Accountability

World-class design teams are built on empowerment, trust, and accountability at every level.

Fail Fast, Fail Safe

I foster a culture of continuous growth, where psychological safety lets teams take risks without losing sight of impact.

Clarity & Connection

Aligning design vision with business strategy so every designer understands not just the task, but the value it creates.

Shared Purpose

Instilling pride, curiosity, and a sense of shared purpose to build teams that are strategic partners, not just executors.

Feedback & Recognition

Giving timely, actionable feedback, celebrating wins, and guiding through challenges so people keep growing.

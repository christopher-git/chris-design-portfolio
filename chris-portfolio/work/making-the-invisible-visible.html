Case Study2024

Making the Invisible Visible: ROI into Retention

Turning abstract ROI data into a tangible "Value Realization" experience that shortened renewal cycles.

Design Leadership · Value Communication

A design-led approach to helping customers see, feel, and trust the value they're getting.

Context & business challenge

Customers weren't perceiving or articulating the value they gained from FourKites. Executives struggled to justify renewals with tangible evidence, renewal cycles were long and difficult with churn risk running high, and upsell conversations lacked strong proof points. The strategic mandate was to elevate value communication from a feature-level conversation to a C-suite business case — positioning FourKites as a trusted partner rather than just a visibility tool.

My role as Director of UX

I framed value realization as both a design challenge — how customers see and feel value — and a business growth lever tied to retention and upsell. I partnered with Sales, CS, and Product leadership to link design outcomes to revenue KPIs (renewals, NPS, churn reduction), directed a multi-disciplinary team across design, product, data science, and marketing, and brought in customer success managers and executive sponsors to co-validate value metrics and storytelling.

Key challenges

Customers used FourKites daily but had no visibility into their own ROI. Renewal cycles ran 8–12 months, and executives stayed unconvinced without data-backed proof. Users disengaged when they couldn't justify cost-to-value, and without a clear value narrative, FourKites struggled to differentiate from competitors at the point that mattered most — renewal.

Strategy & design approach

I defined a value framework to quantify cost savings, workforce productivity, sustainability improvements, and service quality — then borrowed from consumer paradigms like Spotify Wrapped and Strava Recap to make ROI tangible, personal, and habit-forming rather than a once-a-year spreadsheet exercise. Value communication was embedded across three channels: Workspace dashboards for daily visibility, periodic value emails for executive reporting, and sales and renewal collateral for boardroom-ready storytelling — with contextual upsell triggers highlighting how peers benefited from additional FourKites modules.

Execution

I ran a design workshop with cross-functional stakeholders from Sales, CS, Product, and Data Science to brainstorm value communication concepts, then used design benchmarking against gamification patterns in GPay and performance summaries in Strava to validate the approach before guiding the design team through prototypes, executive validation sessions, and pilot rollouts.

Business impact

The initiative equipped users with C-suite-ready metrics, reducing friction in renewal conversations and strengthening trust by consistently demonstrating measurable ROI. Contextual ROI storytelling created new upsell opportunities, and the work positioned FourKites as a business outcome partner rather than just a visibility provider — less a single feature than a shift in how customers perceived FourKites' value, directly influencing retention and upsell.

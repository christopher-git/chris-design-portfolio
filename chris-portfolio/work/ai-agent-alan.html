AI Agent Alan: Automating Logistics Scheduling

Transforming manual appointment coordination into intelligent, 24/7 autonomous operations — FourKites' first autonomous digital worker.

Director of UX, FourKites Intelligent Control Tower · 8-week pilot

Executive summary

I led the UX strategy for AI Agent Alan, FourKites' first autonomous digital worker, built to solve a multi-billion-dollar industry inefficiency in logistics appointment scheduling. Piloted with US Cold Storage across 38 facilities nationwide, the solution achieved an 87% automation success rate while processing 600+ shipments across four major retailers — delivering immediate productivity gains and a scalable framework for AI-driven operational transformation.

The problem

Manual appointment scheduling consumes thousands of labor hours across logistics organizations — every appointment requires repetitive, error-prone coordination over email, portals, and phone calls. US Cold Storage, with 38 facilities nationwide, had identified scheduling as its single largest bottleneck. The design challenge: how might we create an AI agent that behaves like an experienced logistics coordinator — context-aware, reliable, and trustworthy across multi-channel workflows?

Research and design goals

Interviews with schedulers, carrier reps, and logistics managers surfaced that 70% of a scheduler's time went to repetitive coordination, that fragmented portals left no unified visibility, and that users distrust automation that "acts blindly." That research set four non-negotiable design goals: full autonomy from request to confirmation, transparent audit trails for every action, adaptability across diverse portal structures and rescheduling scenarios, and trust built through clear, explainable feedback loops.

The solution

Alan is a multi-channel AI scheduler that automates appointment booking, modification, and confirmation across email workflows, customer portals, and phone-based scheduling systems, integrating with Transportation Management Systems and the FourKites Intelligent Control Tower for real-time data consistency. Core experience features included multi-channel scheduling automation, proactive exception management that reschedules automatically when an ETA changes, intelligent prioritization against business rules, and an audit-trail UI where every action is visible and reversible.

Pilot outcome

Over an 8-week pilot at US Cold Storage, Alan hit an 87% automation success rate and 96% accuracy in securing delivery dates, processing 600+ shipments and saving 36–40 hours weekly per facility. Schedulers described Alan as "invisible but dependable — the best coworker we never hired."

Ethical and reflexive design

Every AI decision is logged and explainable, human-in-the-loop override and audit capabilities are maintained throughout, and the system adheres to enterprise-grade security and data governance. The core lesson: intelligent UX in AI systems isn't about replacing people — it's about elevating human focus from coordination to strategy, and contextual AI only earns trust when it communicates visibly rather than invisibly.

"Alan transformed scheduling from a chore into a conversation — and logistics from reactive coordination into proactive orchestration."

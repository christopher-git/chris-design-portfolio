FourKites Ecosystem: Personas & Journey Mapping

Mapping 10+ personas and end-to-end journeys across FourKites' full product suite to align every team around the customer, not the product they happened to be building.

Design Leadership · UX Research · Cross-Team Alignment · 4 minute read

Problem statement

As a company, we provide multiple products offering to our customers, right from the Real-Time Visibility, Yard Management, Appointment Management, Document Management, Network Visibility, Sustainability and many more.

Challenges

  • There is no clear understanding and empathy with the end-users. There is no place to gain users perspective, their needs, expectation and the journey
  • Every product development happens in a silo, often all our attention to the product we are working on rather than the way our customers view our product. We focus more on products than on customers.
  • There is no end-to-end flow available, which makes our ecosystem looks complex to understand
  • We were unable to tell our story convincingly to our customers on how our product suites span across multiple phases of logistic domains
  • Difficult to create up-sell opportunities
  • Team have no holistic approach on how our system function
  • Gap in the existing system
People work better when they understand the goal and the holistic approach of how things work together.

Goals

  • A clear, cohesive research based personas represent the different user types in the FourKites Ecosystem and their journey
  • Everyone should understand, for whom we are developing the feature and how its going to make their life easy
  • Different products offered by FourKites an its purpose
  • Where in the supply chain these products fit in and how it solve the problems.
  • Different types of persona using our application and their journey
  • The primary use-case/functionality of these products
  • Product flow (end-user journey) of each product
  • How data flow across each product? Are these applications are interconnected? If yes, how?
  • Understanding the future opportunities

Target audience

1. New Joiners

  • A one page overview of FourKites ecosystem
  • Understanding the different product suites offered by FourKites
  • How all the products works together to solve the problem

2. Executives/Marketing/Sales/Product/internal/Ops team

At one place where they can get the overview of entire FourKites ecosystem

  • It help them to understand how every product connects with each other
  • Generate potential opportunities to drive up and cross-selling growth.

3. Those who wanted to deep-dive into a particular product

It help them to understand the product goals, user personals, journeys, primary actions, user flow, and the interoperability.

Discovery

We have had several rounds of discussions with different stakeholders to understand the products and the phase of every product journey. We had many postup activities with different product teams, and we came up with a bunch of ideas on the board.

In the process of building a customer-centric approach, we have created the personas and journey maps for all our personas across different product lines. Having an in-depth understanding of who our customers are, what they do, their needs, and how they interact with our products and etc.,

These persona and user journey helped everyone to easily understand the users' tasks, goals, job nature, opportunities/pain points, technical ability, the tools they use, and etc.,

Takeaways

  • We made this ecosystem as part of the new hire on-boarding bootcamps
  • This ecosystem has been made mandatory at all Executive and Quad meetings.
  • Any integration or addition of new products has been validated with the eco-system on how it integrates into our product lines.
  • Knowledge based training document has been linked to the eco system for better accessibility
  • The product backlog was developed based on opportunity column.
  • Duplication of effort has been eliminated.

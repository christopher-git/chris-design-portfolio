FIN Sim: Simulating Supply Chain Dynamics

A digital-twin simulation experience for stress-testing supply chain decisions before committing to them.

Design Leadership · AI/ML Concept Design · Interaction Design · 2 minute read

Overview

Inspired by the concept of aircraft simulation, where simulators help pilots learn to fly, FIN Sim applies digital-twin concepts to the supply chain world. The goal is to solve complex problems in real time.

FIN Sim utilizes the digital twin concept: virtual representations of physical objects, processes, systems, or environments. These digital twins mirror their real-world counterparts and provide valuable insights and functionalities.

Key Focus:

  • Better Understanding of Complex Systems: Visualize and comprehend intricate supply chain systems with greater clarity.
  • Predicting Problems and Providing Contextual Recommendations: Anticipate issues before they arise and offer actionable solutions.
  • Informed Decision-Making: Make well-informed decisions based on accurate and real-time data from digital twins.
  • Improving Efficiency and Effectiveness: Enhance the operational performance of supply chain processes.
  • Saving Time and Money: Reduce costs and save time by testing and optimizing processes in a virtual environment.

Why this matters

Supply chain planners have historically had to make high-stakes rerouting and contingency decisions with incomplete information and no way to see the downstream consequences until after the fact. FIN Sim gives them a sandbox: run the "what if" before committing to it, the same way a pilot rehearses an emergency procedure long before ever needing it in the air.
